How it works

  1. We confirm your system needs an operatorMany septic systems in North Carolina must have a certified operator. We check yours.
  2. Sign online and we notify the countyWe send you an agreement to read and sign. Once it is signed, we tell the county that an operator is assigned.
  3. We visit and keep you in complianceA certified operator inspects and maintains your system on the schedule the county requires. When something needs repair or pumping, we arrange for a licensed contractor to do the work.
  4. You get the reports, and so does the countyAfter each visit you get a plain-language report on what we found and what to do next, and we file the county’s required report.
