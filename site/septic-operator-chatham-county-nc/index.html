Pittsboro, Siler City, Moncure, Goldston, Bear Creek, Briar Chapel and the Jordan Lake area.

Does your Chatham County system need an operator?

It depends on your system type, printed on your Operation Permit from Chatham County Environmental Health:

What we do for Chatham County owners

  • Inspections on your permit's schedule: tanks, pumps, floats, alarms, the control panel and the field.
  • Reports filed with the county after each visit, with a copy for you.
  • Flow records: 7-day and 30-day flow before each visit when your permit requires it.
  • Violation notices: we work with you, your engineer and the county to get back in compliance.

Systems we see in Chatham County

Drip irrigation (Perc-Rite, Geoflow), low-pressure pipe, pump-to-gravity, E-Z Treat, Orenco AdvanTex and other advanced treatment units.

Common questions from Chatham owners

The county sent me a monitoring report marked "Non-Compliant". What now?

Most items on a county report are routine maintenance. We go through the action items with you, separate what the county requires from what is recommended, take care of what we can, and send the county a current report showing what was done.

My Operation Permit expired. How do I renew it?

Chatham County renews Operation Permits on application. You need a current contract with a certified operator on file. We can help you with the paperwork.
